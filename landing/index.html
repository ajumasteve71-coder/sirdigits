FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still unsure about something? Reach out and a member of the team will walk you through the platform.

Is SirDigits a real trading platform?

Yes. SirDigits is a white-label application built on the Deriv WebSocket API. Trades are executed and settled through Deriv's trading infrastructure, so contract pricing and payouts follow Deriv's rules.

Which markets can I trade?

Digit contracts are offered on synthetic volatility indices, which stream continuously around the clock, including 1-second variants for faster-paced sessions.

Can I practise without risking money?

Absolutely. Every account comes with a demo balance, and you can switch between demo and real accounts instantly from the header without losing your session.

Do I need to install anything?

No. SirDigits runs entirely in the browser on desktop, tablet and mobile. All you need is an internet connection and a Deriv account.

Is there a risk of losing money?

Yes. Trading derivative contracts carries significant risk and you can lose your entire stake. Only trade with money you can afford to lose, and start with the demo account if you are new.
